## B. What the deploy guide must cover

1. **Accounts and cost.** GHCR is free for public images. WorkOS production needs a card on file, but AuthKit is free to 1M monthly active users, and staging is free with no card. Skip the custom AuthKit domain ($99 a month). (workos.com/docs/authkit/environments, /dashboard/billing, /pricing.md; H)
2. **WorkOS production.** Nothing carries over from staging: the issuer, the `/pair` client and every `sub` change, so `TABDOCK_OAUTH_ISSUER` and `TABDOCK_OAUTH_USERS` must be redone. Production redirect URIs must be https, though `http://127.0.0.1` stays allowed, which covers Claude Code's loopback. Secrets show once. Turn on CIMD and DCR, and add `<public URL>/mcp` as the default Resource Indicator. A "Sign up" toggle can close public sign-up, but invitees need accounts (ADR 0016). (environments.md, mcp.md, invite-only-signup.md; H, effect on invites M)
3. **Settings.** Cover every `TABDOCK_*` in `.env.example`, plus the new bind, proxy and audit-path settings. The only true secret is `TABDOCK_PAIR_CLIENT_SECRET`. Set `TABDOCK_ENV=production` and explicit origins; never set the spike or dev-token settings.
4. **DNS.** The hostname needs a public IPv4 A record. Claude refuses private, CGNAT, loopback, mixed and AAAA-only answers. A platform hostname is enough. (claude.com/docs/connectors/building/troubleshooting.md, today; H)
5. **Smoke test.** A POST to `/mcp` should get a 401 that names the metadata URL, the metadata should name the issuer, and `/healthz` should answer 204.
6. **Connector and pages.** Remove the ngrok connector and add `<url>/mcp` once on the web; it cannot be edited later, and phones pick it up (`verified.md`; H). Point adapters at `wss://<host>/page`.
7. **Rollback.** Redeploy the previous digest; never deploy `latest`. Any restart drops attachments, so people pair again. Say whether live invites survive a restart, given the relay keeps their hashes only in memory. WorkOS changes are undone by hand.
8. **Operations.** Logs are JSON on stderr, with secrets redacted by field name (`log.ts`). A provider outage at boot stops startup until the host restarts the relay and it succeeds. Rotate the `/pair` secret at WorkOS first, then on the host. Bump Node before April 2027.

## C. Threat model structure

Use OWASP's four questions and STRIDE per trust boundary, sorting each threat into mitigate, eliminate, transfer or accept (cheatsheetseries.owasp.org Threat Modeling Cheat Sheet; H).

**Assets:** the operator's tab, which acts in their name; page results, plaintext at the relay and at the TLS edge (SPEC section 2); tokens, the `/pair` secret, cookies, codes, nonces, invite secrets and resume tokens; the audit log; the allowlist; the image supply chain; availability.

**Actors:** the operator; members; invitees (any WorkOS account while invites are on); anonymous callers; a script that opens `/page` claiming an allowed origin, which Origin cannot stop outside browsers (ADR 0016); a hostile page or schema; a prompt-injected client; the host and its edge; WorkOS; npm, base images and Actions.

**Boundaries:** internet to edge; edge to container (plaintext, the trusted address header); Claude to `/mcp` (both legs) and metadata; phone to `/pair` and `/i`; page to `/page`; adapter to page tools; relay to WorkOS; relay to the audit volume; CI to GHCR to host. Give each boundary one STRIDE row naming its S-numbers and test files.

**Residual risks and trade-offs:**

- The argument check is advisory: it can be made to skip, it never runs regexes, `format` or `uniqueItems`, and pages must check their own arguments. A per-page schema memory budget is still open (ADRs 0008, 0010; backlog).
- The relay-wide `/pair` sign-in budget lets anyone who knows the URL stall QR sign-in. Pairing by code from Claude is unaffected. A per-address share waits on A9 (ADR 0013 notes).
- The host's edge sees plaintext, as ngrok did (ADR 0014).
- The allowlist is the real gate, since sign-up is open (ADRs 0013, 0016).
- `/page` takes no credentials, so only the address and total caps bound it, and address caps mean something only after A9 (ADR 0009 notes).
- On the polyfill, a write can hold the page's slot until its deadline plus 2 s (ADR 0012).
- Restarts lose all state, an availability cost.
- Cite the review history: M2's session flooding, tools-frame budget and validator blow-up, and M3's one medium and twelve lows, each fixed with a test (`docs/plans/M2.md`, `M3.md`).
