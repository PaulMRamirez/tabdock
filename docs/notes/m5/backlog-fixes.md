# Backlog fixes for M5: the five small items and the residuals

Checked on 5 October 2026 on branch `m5` in a scratch copy, where each issue failed a probe test before its fix and passed after; the repo is unchanged. With all five small fixes together, `vitest run` passed 1358 tests in 83 files. Outside behaviour was probed live in Chromium 141 and Node 22.22.0, and read in `ws` 8.22.0, `@modelcontextprotocol/node` 2.1.1 and zod 4.6.5, all still `latest`.

## The five small items

**Request shares (`config.ts:819`).** Each share is checked against `MIN_REQUEST_BYTES` but not against the other: `TABDOCK_MAX_REQUEST_BYTES=8388608` with the default 24 MiB per-user share resolves. `relay.ts` answers 413 unparsed only past the per-user share, so a body charged between the two is parsed and then refused `page_busy`. Fix: after that loop, throw when `requestBytesPerUser > requestBytes`, naming both variables, and say so in `.env.example`. Test: in `m4-interfaces.test.ts`, the total alone at 8 MiB is refused naming `TABDOCK_MAX_REQUEST_BYTES_PER_USER`; equal values pass.

**Expired invite approved (`hub.ts:2364`).** `#approveRedemption` checks the invite exists, the bar, the sponsor and the seats, never `expiresAt`. Probe: a guest redeems a control invite, the clock passes its end before its timer runs, the page allows, and the guest is attached. Fix: after the guard at 2382, an invite with `expiresAt <= Date.now()` goes through `#closeInvite(invite, 'expired')` and the request, if still open, ends `pairing_expired`, as the sponsor branch does. Test: that probe in `invites.test.ts`, asserting `invite_closed` with reason `expired` and no attachment.

**Relay deadline in the adapter (`core.ts:1839`, `1852`).** The schema takes any positive safe integer. Chromium 141 ran a 2^31 ms timer at once and wrapped 2^32+5000 to 5 s; Node sets such a timer to 1 ms. Probe: with harness timers behaving as Chromium's, an invoke with `deadlineMs` 2^31 answered `timeout` within 1 ms. `holdUnwatched` (2153) adds 2 s, so it overflows near the maximum too and frees the polyfill's write slot early. Fix: `deadlineMs = Math.min(frame.deadlineMs, MAX_TIMER_MS - UNWATCHED_HANDLER_GRACE_MS)` for the deadline and its timer, with `MAX_TIMER_MS` moved from `config.ts:384` to the protocol's constants. A schema `.max()` would make an odd deadline a reconnect loop. Test: in `core.test.ts`, deadlines of 2^31 and `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` give no result after 1 s, `timeout` past the cap, and no delay over 2^31-1 reaches `timers.setTimeout`.

**"Upper bound" (`request-heap.ts:42`).** Measured on both legs: a `_meta` of one key `"4294967294"` then 190,000 short integer keys, which V8 keeps as dictionary elements, held 52.4 MB against 46.9 MB charged on the 2026-07-28 leg (1.12; 0.85 on the 2025-era leg), against 0.94 for the old worst shape. So the gap is about 12%, not 5%. Options: reword only, or reword and add a surcharge. Recommended: both. `INDEX_KEY_HEAP_BYTES = 128` more for each key of digits alone (flag a digits-only string at its closing quote, add on the colon; about 15 lines) brought that shape to 0.74. Reword it as "measured above the heap of every shape in `call-shapes.ts`" wherever "upper bound" describes this charge, ADR 0018's notes and threat model B1 included. Test: add the shape to `CALL_SHAPES`; `call-heap.test.ts` runs every shape on both legs and fails on the modern leg without the surcharge.

**Invoke into a closing socket (`hub.ts:4265`).** `#access` refuses a socket the relay is closing, but one the page closes stays live in `CLOSING` until ws reports the close, up to ws's 30 s `closeTimeout` if the peer keeps TCP open, and ws then sends nothing. Probe: after a page sent a close frame and kept TCP open, a `get_view` waited its 3 s deadline and wrote a full `call` line as if delivered. Fix: before encoding, a socket closing or not `OPEN` settles the call `page_asleep`; the probe then answered in 12 ms, its line within the refusal budget. Test: that probe in `page-link.test.ts`.

## The availability residuals

**Guests share the relay-wide request total.** True: `#holdBytes` (`hub.ts:2900`) ignores the account's kind. Three guests calling a page that stops reading (a frozen tab) hold 24 MiB each for 45 s at a time, so members get `page_busy` everywhere. Medium-low: it needs an invite and is attributed. Fix in M5: invitees together hold at most a quarter of `requestBytes` (never under `MIN_REQUEST_BYTES`), refused `page_busy` for a call and `rate_limited` for a pairing, the rank rule ADR 0016's notes apply to sessions. Test in `request-bytes.test.ts`: guests fill the quarter, the next guest call is refused, a member's is still held.

**A full `/pair` store evicts guests' `/i` sessions.** True (`pair.ts:454`). Low: someone partway through `/i` looks like a stranger until they redeem, and B2 already accepts six addresses stalling `/i` sign-in for everyone. Accept; B2's residual adds that a full store (200) ends the oldest non-member session first, a guest's at `/i` included.

**Results a client leaves unread.** True: Node 22's `server.timeout` is 0, the SDK's Node adapter waits for `drain` without limit, and the hub releases a call's charge before its response is written. Probe: a client that stopped reading left a response at `writableLength` 262,153 and `writableNeedDrain` true, unending. Any member, or a guest of a page with long results, can leave 240 a minute unread (60 for a guest), each up to 120,000 characters plus a structured copy. Medium: the only residual here with no time bound. Fix in M5: `relay.ts` tracks open `/mcp` responses and checks them every 10 s, destroying one whose `writableLength` is above zero and has not fallen for three checks, with one line per kind a window; a client that reads, however slowly, is never cut (ADR 0024's rule for pages). Test: the check against a stand-in response with fake timers (loopback absorbed 3.5 MiB, more than one result), plus its wiring.

**Unbudgeted 2026-07-28 requests.** True: 50 `tools/list` on a budget of 3, then `list_pages` still answered, and page tools make each list heavier (`page-tools.md`). Medium-low. Fix in M5: in `modern` (`relay.ts:510`), each parsed request other than `tools/call` and `subscriptions/listen` spends one before the SDK sees it, else 429 with the budget's refusal and the request's id. In the copy this refused the third request; three tests in `mcp-log-budget.test.ts` need new counts, and the probe becomes a test.

**Credential-free pages and the tool budget.** True. A page is charged at most 5.36 MB (`relisted`), so about 12 pages fill 64 MiB: three address keys in hosted mode, one elsewhere, after which any page that relists is closed with 1008. Cheaper than B3's 20 keys for every slot, but the same class (a /48 holds 256 keys). Accept; add these numbers to B4's residual and a "later" row for ranking pages with a member attached.

**Rosters from pairing retries.** True, but retries are capped at 30 a minute per page, so 256 counted frames take over eight minutes of a link accepting nothing, long after the 30 s silence close. Very low. Accept, one clause in B4.

**Look-alike emails.** True: `foldName` (`auth.ts:109`) folds NFKC, case and invisible characters, not cross-script look-alikes, which need Unicode's confusables data (a new dependency or a vendored table). The widget always shows the invited badge and short id, as S14 asks. Low. Accept; B5's residual names it.
