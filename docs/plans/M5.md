# M5 plan: first-class tools, both revisions, confirmation in the client and release 0.1.0

Goal: with `TABDOCK_FIRST_CLASS_TOOLS` on, members' clients on both MCP revisions list and call each attached page's tools by name, through the very path `call_page_tool` uses, and hear of changes to their own pages only (A5.1); both revisions are verified by a client matrix and the official conformance suite in CI and by Claude Code in the sandbox (A5.2); a page that opts in lets its member drivers confirm consequential calls in their own clients, while every other case still prompts on the page (A5.3); the MCP-B comparison and the upstream draft exist and the adapter passes its specs on the MCP-B 6 beta (A5.4); 0.1.0 is packed, installed and started in CI as three npm packages, and the tour site builds (A5.5); and a separate agent's section 9 review leaves no high-severity issue open (A5.6). ADRs 0025 to 0031 make every SPEC change, accepted on 5 October under the owner's standing instruction of 4 October, and ADR 0032 the changes the Step 1 review called for. The decisions are in `docs/notes/m5/m5-decisions.md`, the research beside it, the facts in `docs/notes/verified.md`.

## Decisions in brief

D1 (ADR 0025): the whole tool surface moves to the SDK's low-level `Server`; first-class names are `<page id>__<tool>`, listed per user by role, each description behind a relay prefix that calls it untrusted, cached privately on 2026-07-28, with `list_changed` sent only to the user's own sessions and listen streams; invitees keep the five fixed tools, and the flag is off by default everywhere. D2 (ADR 0026): with `policy.confirmVia: 'client'`, a member driver on an attachment no invite made, whose client declares form elicitation, confirms a consequential call there, bound to one exact call by the SDK's request state codec and a single-use record on 2026-07-28 and by an elicitation inside the request on a 2025-era session; the adapter honours the invoke's `confirmation` only under its own policy, rule and grants, and every failure answers `not_confirmed`. D3 (ADR 0027): the relay claims 2025-03-26 to 2026-07-28 (2024-11-05 best effort), `/mcp` refuses a present `Origin` off its allowlist, `-32022` lists every revision served, and one `mcp client` line per user, client and leg shows what hosted Claude speaks. D4 (ADR 0031, ADR 0001's notes): the demo stays on MCP-B 5.1.0 while a Playwright leg runs the 6.0 beta, `tabdock.v1` stays, and the owner posts the upstream draft. D5 (ADR 0028): `@tabdock/protocol`, `@tabdock/adapter` and `@tabdock/relay` at 0.1.0, the relay an esbuild bundle with one bin, `tabdock-relay`, the protocol on `zod/mini`, a `headersHelper` banner on POSIX shells, stage-only trusted publishing. D6 (ADR 0029): `apps/site` publishes the demo at `/` and the tour at `/tour/` through `pages.yml`, Mermaid drawn at build time, and the demo dials only a relay its visitor clicks to connect. D7 (ADR 0030): the five small fixes and three new S9 limits. D8 (ADR 0031): section 3 refreshed, A5.1 to A5.6 added.

Section 9 changes: S6 (a page may opt in to confirmation in the client), S7 (`confirmedBy`), S9 (the first-class list caps, ADR 0030's three limits, the byte charge of a request waiting on a client's confirmation, and from ADR 0032 each invitee's share and the cut-off's measure), S10 (the first-class description prefix) and S12 (`Host` and `Origin` on `/mcp`). None weakens a requirement; the A5.6 reviewer checks the one new trust ADR 0026 records, that an opted-in page takes the relay's word that a member driver confirmed.

New dev dependencies outside CLAUDE.md's list, accepted under the standing instruction and named in the pull request: `@modelcontextprotocol/conformance` 0.2.0-alpha.12 and the v1 SDK aliases in `tests/e2e`, the MCP-B 6.0 beta aliases in their own private package `tests/mcpb6` (ADR 0032), and `marked` and `mermaid` in `apps/site`, which keeps its own lockfile outside the workspace. No new runtime dependency.
