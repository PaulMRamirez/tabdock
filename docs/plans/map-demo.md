# Map demo plan: Earth on the Pages site

Goal: a public page at `/map/` that shows what Tabdock does for a map. A visitor connects a relay of their own, pairs Claude or another client through the widget, and watches it move a flat map and a globe of Earth, take a short tour, capture what it sees and follow the page's state. It is the map profile (ADR 0038) in a presentable page, under ADR 0050's constraints and size budget, and `tests/map-page` stays CI's fixture, untouched.

## Files

`apps/map-demo` is `@tabdock/map-demo`, private, in the workspace (`pnpm-workspace.yaml` names it beside `apps/demo`). Dependencies: `@mcp-b/webmcp-polyfill` 5.1.0, `@tabdock/adapter`, `@tabdock/sim-page` and `@tabdock/demo`, all workspace or exact; esbuild as a dev dependency. `index.html` holds the markup and one inline style. Under `src/`: `main.ts` wires polyfill, world, tools, page and link; `earth.ts` holds the world's data; `data/natural-earth-110m.ts` is generated; `decode.ts` reads it; `projection.ts`, `draw.ts`, `capture.ts` and `gestures.ts` draw; `connect.ts` is the bar and form; `panel.ts` the tour panel, prompts and call strip; `webmcp.ts` the WebMCP slice it uses. Under `scripts/`: `server.ts` (dev server and `buildMapDemo()`, exported as `./server`) and `vendor-natural-earth.ts`. Tests sit beside their modules. `apps/demo` gains two exports so the map reuses the board's code rather than copying it: `./relay` (`checkRelayUrl`) and `./arming` (`armWhenStill`, moved unchanged out of `connect.ts`); its `staticIndexHtml` takes the policy as an argument, the board's by default.

## Data

`node apps/map-demo/scripts/vendor-natural-earth.ts`, run by hand and by no CI job, fetches `ne_110m_land.geojson` and `ne_110m_lakes.geojson` from `raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/`, stops unless their SHA-256 are `9e0729ee...35d9` and `eb02ecc8...ffc9` (in full in the script), and parses them with zod. It quantizes to tenths of a degree, drops repeated positions and rings under four, writes exterior rings counterclockwise and holes clockwise as RFC 7946 asks (the files hold shapefile order), delta codes each ring as zigzag varints in base64, and keeps each lake's name and rank. The Caspian Sea is the land layer's one hole; the script names it only after checking it lies within 46 to 55 E and 36 to 48 N. It writes `src/data/natural-earth-110m.ts`, formatted by prettier's API, opening with the source URLs, tag, commit `f1890d9`, hashes and "generated; do not edit", and exporting `ATTRIBUTION`, "Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com." The same input gives the same bytes, so a rerun at the tag shows no diff. Expected size about 15,500 bytes, under the 24,000 budget.

## Renderer

The world's viewport is fixed at 960 by 540; the screen canvas is its CSS width times `devicePixelRatio`, drawn through a scale, so neither tools nor captures depend on the window. The flat map is the profile's local equirectangular projection (`geometry.ts`), drawn without its longitude wrap in three copies 360 degrees apart: Natural Earth cuts rings at the antimeridian, so each copy is a plain polygon and the canvas clips the rest. The globe is orthographic: rings are densified to 2 degree steps, points past the limb are carried onto it, a ring wholly hidden is skipped, and a ring whose lon and lat outline holds the view's antipode adds the limb circle under the even-odd rule, so a continent wrapping behind the globe fills only its visible edge. Coast strokes follow visible runs and skip the edges the data adds along 180 degrees and the South Pole. Order: ocean, land, lakes, graticule, outlines, names, north arrow. Features and outlines go through `projectToScreen`, so the tools and the canvas agree. It follows the fixture's draw order, capture fit loop and gestures, extended, but imports nothing from `tests/map-page`. It reads no clock, random number or window size, and sets its colours and one font once, so one view captures to the same PNG bytes within a browser.

## World

Earth, radius 6,371,008.8 m (IUGG mean); projections `equirectangular` and `globe`; no clock, so no clock group. Layers: `land` (base), `lakes`, `graticule`, `outlines` and `names`. Features are the 24 lakes at label points the generator computes, the Caspian Sea, and Madagascar. One walkthrough, `earth`, of six stops, each fact checked when written: Earth from afar on the globe (water covers about 71 percent of the surface); the Caspian Sea on the flat map (the largest inland body of water by area, about 371,000 square kilometres, a hole in the land here); Lake Baikal on the globe (the deepest lake, about 1,642 metres); the Great Lakes on the flat map (Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake by area); Lake Victoria on the flat map (Africa's largest lake by area, which the Victoria Nile leaves); Madagascar on the globe (the fourth largest island). Three observations, kind `outline`, outline the Caspian, Baikal and the Great Lakes from the data, dated 2022-05-13T00:00:00.000Z (v5.1.2), sourced to Natural Earth with a note that each is an outline, not a photograph. Nothing names a border, a country's claim, a person or a brand.

## Policy and page

`MAP_PAGE_POLICY` with `imageTools: ['capture_view']`: `reset_edits` alone is consequential; proposals off, invites and drivers at the adapter's defaults; nothing read from the link. The page, one column: a title and one line of what it is, with links to the board, the tour and guide page 16; the Connect bar or form, with a line saying the relay must admit this site's origin (`TABDOCK_ALLOWED_ORIGINS`) and that a browser may ask before a public page reaches a loopback relay; the status line; the canvas with buttons for zoom, flat or globe and the previous or next stop, since a phone has no wheel; the view line; the tour panel (stop, title, text, written as text only); four example prompts to paste ("Take this page's tour and capture each stop", "Show Lake Baikal on the globe from 2,000 km up, then capture it", "Hide the graticule and list the lakes in view", "Rewrite this stop's text for a ten-year-old"); the call strip, tool and outcome only; and the credit. The remembered choice uses its own key, `tabdock-map-demo:connect:`.

## State

As the fixture: once linked the page publishes `world.state()`, then again on each discrete change, skipping a value whose JSON is unchanged; a drag, wheel step or button is one write.

## Build and dev

`buildMapDemo({ outDir })` empties `outDir`, bundles minified with a source map, and writes `index.html` with the policy ADR 0050 names. `apps/site/scripts/build.ts` calls it after `buildDemo` into `dist/map`; a new `scriptedPageProblems()` in `apps/site/lib/checks.ts` reads `/` and `/map/` back (one policy meta ahead of all it governs, the no-referrer meta, one relative script, no inline script or event attribute), `/map/` must hold exactly `index.html`, `main.js`, `main.js.map` and the recording's two files in `media/` within budget, and the header and tour index link the demo. Root scripts: `dev:map-demo` (port 5175 or `MAP_DEMO_PORT`, printing a `?relay=` link for `pnpm relay`) and `record:map-demo`. `site-files.test.ts`'s workspace list gains the app.

## Tests

Unit, in Node: decoding (a round trip, the vendored counts, closure, ranges, budget, credit); projection (flat copies equal `projectToScreen` within 180 degrees of the centre and under heading; the limb, a hidden ring, an antipode ring, skipped cut edges); `earth.ts` (parses under `MapWorldDataSchema`, every stop and footprint over drawn data, texts within caps); the capture fit loop on a stand-in canvas; gestures; and the choice key, apart from the board's. Playwright, `tests/e2e/specs/map-demo.spec.ts` (`tests/e2e` takes the app as a workspace dev dependency), on the static build served as a static host would: no policy violation; the fourteen tools registered; no dial from `?relay` or a script's click; a trusted click, pairing through a local relay and approval with `@tabdock/adapter/testing`; the tour walked by `go_to_stop`, with published state read after each stop; one view captured twice to the same PNG and another to other bytes; `reset_edits` prompts; 375 px wide with the widget open and no sideways scroll. Site: `checks.test.ts` cases for a missing policy, an inline script, an extra file and an oversized bundle.

## Recording

`pnpm record:map-demo` (`tests/e2e/src/record-map-demo.ts`, never in CI) builds the page as the site holds it and serves it on a loopback static host, starts `pnpm relay` in local mode with a throwaway `TABDOCK_HOME` whose `TABDOCK_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` names that origin, opens the page at 1280 by 720 with Playwright's video on, clicks the Connect bar, approves in the widget, and has an SDK client holding the owner token list the walkthrough, go to each stop with a pause, read `get_page_state` once and call `capture_view` once, the picture it returned shown in an inset, then `set_view` back out to the globe, under a caption bar naming each step, for about 55 seconds. A shade covers the widget's lane before the Connect click, the code's block is hidden inside the widget before the shade lifts, and each scene checks that neither the code nor its QR code has a box on screen, so no frame shows either; the run prints only file paths and sizes. It writes the take, its poster (the last scene) and a still of each scene into the ignored `apps/map-demo/recording/` for review, and copies a take within budget, 4,000,000 bytes for the video and 300,000 for the poster, into `apps/map-demo/media/`, which `buildMapDemo` copies beside the page, the page shows as a poster linking the video, and the README shows linking the site's `/map/` (ADR 0050's notes from the recording). `tests/e2e/test/record-map-demo.test.ts` holds the review files ignored and the kept ones tracked, of their kind and within budget. It films an SDK client; a video of Claude itself is the owner's step 4 in `docs/checklists/map-demo.md`.

## Risks

Globe clipping near the antipode, held by unit cases and captures at several views. Visitors' relays must admit the site's origin and the browser may prompt for a loopback relay; the page says so. The bundle carries the adapter, the polyfill and zod, as the fixture's 679,970 bytes do; over budget means trimming, never a silent raise. A stop's fact could be wrong; each is checked when written and rounded with "about". Two pages with script share the origin (ADR 0050). Fixes to the fixture's renderer, fit loop or gestures may be owed here. Fonts differ by machine, so captures are compared within one run, never against committed bytes. Pages caches for 600 s, so a fix shows within ten minutes.
