# 0049: Refresh SPEC after M6's docs pass

Status: Accepted, 12 October 2026, under the owner's standing instruction to take the recommended option. Changes SPEC's dated line and the wording of sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 11, and writes into S9 the refusal of animated images that ADR 0039's note of 12 October decided. Changes no behaviour beyond that note: decision 4's changes are words a person or a client reads, and two guards in the tests. Sets a convention for dated notes (decision 2) and says which release carries M6 (decision 3). Amends no earlier ADR's Decision; ADRs 0029, 0031 and 0038 to 0047 gain notes or pointers.

## Context

The docs pass of 12 October read the whole documentation set against the code at `d81e8c8`, M6 as merged with its section 9 review (A6.28). SPEC was last amended on 10 October by ADRs 0038 to 0048, and the notes those records gained through 11 and 12 October have changed SPEC sections and S rules in place since. ADR 0036 made the last such refresh, for 0.1.0, as a record of its own, and this one follows it. Most of the contract holds: the nine fixed tools, every frame and its fields, the codes, the limits and the fourteen S rules match the code. About twenty sentences had drifted or were silent where a reader would trip. Section 3 lacked three facts M6 rests on, all already rows of `docs/notes/verified.md`. Section 4 named neither `pnpm ci-check` nor the digest that binds the snapshot to the `/pair` client. Section 5's session row mixed what the relay keeps with what the adapter stores. Section 6's `session_end` row lacked its `reason`, and its `attach_request` row named fewer of the prompts that carry an agent token than S11 does. Section 7 said `detach_page` ends what the caller's invites let in, but not that it closes their invites and agent tokens; it said a wait answers once the version passes `after`, where the relay answers on any difference; and it counted a page's watching seats among its waits with invites off too. Section 8 listed fewer armed controls than the widget arms, and forgot that the tab also stores revokes the relay has not yet applied. Section 11 named neither `pnpm ci-check` nor `pnpm measure:room` among what a cloud session runs.

Two questions of record also needed an answer. ADR 0031's Consequences say "notes cannot change SPEC", and the backlog repeats it, yet the notes of ADRs 0039 to 0047 have changed SPEC sections and S rules, each naming the line it changed, while their status lines still list only what the Decision changed. And guide page 17 installed `@tabdock/adapter@0.1.0` for M6's testing helper, and the package READMEs described M6's features beside "Version 0.1.0 is prepared", while the CHANGELOG, ADR 0045's decision 10 and the M5 checklist plan 0.1.0 as M5's code.

## Decision

**1. SPEC wording.** Each quote is what SPEC now says.

The dated line: "last amended 12 October 2026 by ADRs 0038 to 0049 and their notes".

Section 3's heading reads "Facts verified on 2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 11 and 12 October 2026", and three rows gain a fact each, taken from `docs/notes/verified.md`. MCP-B: "5.1.0's polyfill, its dist read again on 12 October, still settles `executeTool` as soon as the caller's signal aborts while the handler runs on unaware, which section 8's image gate allows for by giving the call that holds it a signal nothing aborts (ADR 0039's notes)". Claude Code: "Claude Code 2.1.296, checked on 11 October, runs headless within flags, which ADR 0041's advisory walk relies on: `--tools ""` leaves only MCP tools, `--setting-sources project` run from an empty directory loads none of the user's settings files, allow rules or hooks while still sending the sign-in, and `--strict-mcp-config`, `--allowedTools` and `--permission-mode dontAsk` hold it to the servers and tools given". Fly: "`kill_timeout` ... is in seconds, 5 by default and at most 300, and best effort; `deploy/fly/fly.toml` sets 30, since stopping the fullest hosted relay and writing its 12 MB restart snapshot took 6.2 to 8.7 s at the baseline (ADR 0046)". The sentence under the table names this record.

Section 4: the `scripts/` line names "pnpm ci-check (ci-check.ts, ADR 0041)" and `public-mode.ts`, and the snapshot is applied only when written "under the same `/pair` client (bound by a domain-separated digest of the `/pair` client's id and secret, never the secret itself)" (`credentialsDigest` in `packages/relay/src/snapshot.ts`).

Section 5: the time-boxed session's fields are "`sessionId`, `startedAt`, `endsAt`, `lengthMs`, `policy` (`maxDrivers`, `proposals`), as the relay keeps it; the adapter's stored record adds `label`, `observers`, `inviteIds` and `listed`" (`TimedSession` in `store.ts`; `StoredSessionSchema` in `packages/protocol/src/storage.ts`).

Section 6: `session_end` carries "`sessionId`, `reason?` (`'time'`)", which "the relay records as `time` only when its own end is at most a minute away (`SESSION_CLOCK_SLACK_MS`), and as the operator's otherwise (ADR 0043's notes)". `attach_request`'s agent prompt is "its first, the first after a prompt for it timed out or the page dropped one, and the first after a restart snapshot brought the token back" (ADR 0044's notes, S11). The proposal place hold already names a proposer's detach, and stands.

Section 7: `detach_page` "removes the caller's own attachment, closes the invites and agent tokens they sponsored on the page and ends every attachment those let in (S14) ...; an agent's own detach closes its token" (`#loseSponsors` in `hub.ts`). `wait_for_page_state` answers "as soon as the version differs from `after` (at once if it already does, ahead or behind; ADR 0040, decision 8)", as `hub.ts` compares `now.version !== after`. A page's waits are "its people limit plus, with invites on, its watching seats" (`waitLimitsOf` in `waits.ts`). The label paragraph reads "only a still PNG, JPEG or WebP".

Section 8: the armed controls are "Prompts, roster rows (the role switch and Revoke on every row), proposal rows, the pause switch, the Invite form, the agent command's Copy and Done and each agent token's Cancel, the session form and a running session's New link, Extend and End, the Watching section's toggle, the box that asks before a Revoke, and the record's save, discard and offer boxes", while "Revoke all and an invite's Cancel, which only take access away, and Dismiss all, which runs nothing, are not held back" (the `boxButton` and `button` calls in `widget.ts`). The tab's `sessionStorage` also keeps "the revokes the relay has not yet applied" (the seven storage keys in `core.ts`).

S9 gains: "An animated PNG (an `acTL` chunk before its first `IDAT`) or WebP (the VP8X animation flag, or an `ANIM` or `ANMF` chunk) is no still image and is refused, since a file within the byte cap could decode to far more pixels than the dimension caps allow (ADR 0039's notes)", and its image clause reads "one still PNG, JPEG or WebP image". No other S rule changes.

Section 11: a cloud session's tests are joined by "`pnpm ci-check` and, with root and about 75 minutes, `pnpm measure:room`, which records how it held the relay to 512 MiB and one CPU where cgroup v1 groups are missing (A6.22)".

**2. Notes may amend.** A dated note in an ADR may amend its own record's Decision, or SPEC, when it names the decision or the SPEC line it changes and says why, as the notes of ADRs 0039 to 0047 have done. The record's status line then lists every SPEC section and S rule its notes changed, after what its Decision changed ("Its notes also change ..."), so a reader of the status line alone learns every place the record reaches. A note never weakens a section 9 requirement, and a change of design still takes a record of its own. ADR 0031's "since notes cannot change SPEC" and the backlog's copy of it give way to this rule.

**3. Which release carries M6.** 0.1.0 is M5's code. Its `v0.1.0` tag goes on `main`'s last commit before the M6 merge, `6ab8389` (main's head on 12 October), which stays on `main`, so `publish.yml`'s check that the tagged commit is an ancestor of `origin/main` passes. M6 ships in the release after 0.1.0, expected to be 0.2.0, since the CHANGELOG's rule for 0.x versions lets a minor version change the page link and M6 adds frames to it. Documents say so and write `<version>` where an install needs M6. If 0.1.0 is on npm when that release is prepared, ADR 0045's decision 10 first moves `AUDIT_VERSION` to 2 with a reader for both. `docs/release.md` says the same.

**4. Small changes to product text.** Made in this pass, none touching section 9: the relay's MCP instructions name page state reads, proposals and an agent's `pair_page` with no arguments; `pair_page`'s and `detach_page`'s descriptions gain a clause for an agent, and an agent's empty `list_pages` says to call `pair_page` with no arguments (`packages/relay/src/mcp.ts`); the widget gives each refusal of a session a sentence of its own and says that New link closes the session's earlier links (`packages/adapter/src/widget.ts`); `/i` says to add the connector in Claude on the web or the desktop app (`packages/relay/src/pair-page/invite.html`); `tests/ci-check/playwright.config.ts` stops at once when `CI_CHECK_PAGE_URL` is not set; and `deploy-files.test.ts` refuses `TABDOCK_MEMBERS_FILE` under `fly.toml`'s `[env]`, since the deploy workflow alone chooses which of it and `TABDOCK_OAUTH_USERS` the relay gets and unsets the other.

## Consequences

SPEC again says what M6's code does, a status line names every place its record reaches, and a reader of the guide, the CHANGELOG and the package READMEs meets one answer about which release carries M6. Section 9 changes only by ADR 0039's note, which adds a refusal and so weakens nothing; every other S rule reads as before. Decision 4 changes words a client's model and the operator read, and no check, limit or role. Section 3 stays a dated record that each milestone refreshes from `verified.md`, now by a record or by a note that names its line.
