# M6 checklist

Written on 11 October 2026 in Wave 3 of `docs/plans/M6.md`, on branch `m6` as Waves 1 and 2 and Wave 3's record, snapshot and room measurement left it (`8b8e4e7`), before Wave 3's integration and the A6.28 review; the integration of 12 October added its results here, and the review's followed the same day. What the sandbox showed comes first, mapped to SPEC section 10's A6.1 to A6.28; then the steps only you can take, grouped by where you take them. Each says what to run and what to send me or record; I put what you send in `docs/notes/verified.md` with its date unless the step names another place. (C) marks a step, or the part of one, that needs a computer, as every step under On your computer does; everything else works in a phone browser. Placeholders are M4's: `<domain>` your personal domain, `<app>` the Fly app name.
