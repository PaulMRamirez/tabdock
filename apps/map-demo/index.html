Earth, on a page your MCP client can drive

Earth as a flat map and a globe, drawn on a plain canvas from Natural Earth's public-domain data with no map library. Connect a Tabdock relay of your own, pair Claude or another MCP client in the widget, and ask it to take the tour. No relay yet? Use the buttons under the map to take the tour yourself.

Watch a client take the tour

A recording under a minute long of this page in a browser, beside a pane listing each call as it went. The client is a script on the MCP SDK standing in for Claude, not Claude: it pairs through a relay on the same machine, is allowed in as driver, and takes the tour, capturing one view and reading the page's state. Every call and answer shown is real, the captions say what a person would ask, and the pairing code stays off camera. Open the MP4 (under 4 MB).
